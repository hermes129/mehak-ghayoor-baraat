# Mehak & Ghayoor — the Baraat

The client's invitation on the **Minimal Ivory Nikkah** template, which they
chose over the compass-and-rule design (kept in git as the tag
`compass-and-rule`).

Mr. and Mrs. Syed Hassam Uddin invite guests to the baraat of their
daughter Mehak with Ghayoor Raza, son of Mr. and Mrs. Raza Hussain Kazmi.
Friday 8 January 2027, arrival 9 pm, Royal Sapphire, Plot # B-54, 13B,
Block 1, Gulistan-e-Johar, Karachi.

## What changed from the template

- Every name, date, time, venue and the countdown target are the client's.
- The **attire** section is gone: the client asked for no dress code.
- The **programme** holds Arrival of Baraat (9 pm) and Rukhsati (11 pm).
- The **music** is the client's choice: the opening bar of "Tere Bin" from
  Wazir (2016, Sonu Nigam and Shreya Ghoshal), from 1.032 to 9.032 s, an exact
  8 s musical bar, cut before the wavering line that enters at about 10.5 s.
  Raised 7.6 dB, repeated to 104 s with 1.5 s fades at the file's ends. This is
  a commercial recording supplied by the client's side, not openly licensed.
- The **RSVP form** is gone. Replies go to the family by call or WhatsApp
  (prefilled message): Syed Adil, Syed Mohib and Osama Ahmed; Syed Danish
  is listed by name, as no number was given.
- The cover names are sized for one line on phones: "Mehak & Ghayoor" ran off
  both edges at the template's 17vw.
- The calendar entry runs 9 pm to midnight.

The photographs are the template's and show no faces. The venue photograph
is atmosphere, not Royal Sapphire itself.
